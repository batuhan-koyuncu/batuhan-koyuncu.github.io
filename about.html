My name is Batuhan Koyuncu. I create lecture notes, problem solutions, visual explanations, and computational examples for students in physics, mathematics, electrical engineering, and programming.

Teaching style

My teaching focuses on step-by-step explanations, mathematical clarity, and connecting abstract theory with concrete examples. I especially like building intuition through derivations, diagrams, and computational plots.

Subjects

  • Physics and mathematical physics
  • Vector calculus and differential equations
  • Electrodynamics and quantum mechanics
  • Electrical engineering and control theory
  • Python, JavaScript, GitHub, and LaTeX workflows

Purpose of this website

This website collects free educational content such as lecture PDFs, worked examples, Python-generated plots, hand-drawn figures, and study guides. The goal is to make difficult topics easier to study and to help students decide whether private tutoring would be useful for them.
