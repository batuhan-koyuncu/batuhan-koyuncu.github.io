About

MSc-level theoretical physics tutoring with a focus on clarity.

My name is Batuhan Koyuncu. I create lecture notes, problem sheets, computational examples and exam-oriented explanations for students in physics, mathematics, electrical engineering, programming, data analysis, statistics, business and taxation.

Approach

Derivations before memorization.

I emphasize definitions, notation, assumptions and the chain of reasoning behind each formula. The goal is to make students comfortable with the method, not only with the final answer.

International support

For university and school students.

I support university students, German teenagers, international students in Germany and exam candidates preparing for Abitur, A Level or SAT.

Teaching style

Mathematical clarity, physical intuition and computation.

Step-by-step reasoning

Problems are broken into definitions, assumptions, equations, intermediate steps and final interpretation.

Visual explanations

Diagrams, sketches and plots are used when they make an abstract construction easier to understand.

Computational methods

Python simulations, notebooks and plots connect theory with quantitative behaviour and model limitations.
