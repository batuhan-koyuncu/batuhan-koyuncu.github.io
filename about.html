About

MSc-level theoretical physics tutoring with a focus on clarity.

My name is Batuhan Koyuncu. I create lecture notes, problem solutions, visual explanations, and computational examples for students in physics, mathematics, electrical engineering, and programming.

Approach

Derivations before memorization.

I emphasize definitions, notation, assumptions, and the chain of reasoning behind each formula. The goal is to make students comfortable with the method, not only with the final answer.

International students

Flexible academic support.

The website is written for students from different university systems who need clear technical explanations, exam-oriented practice, and support with academic workflows.

Teaching style

Mathematical clarity, intuition, and computation.

Step-by-step reasoning

Problems are broken into definitions, assumptions, equations, intermediate steps, and final interpretation.

Visual explanations

Diagrams, sketches, and plots are used when they make an abstract construction easier to understand.

Computational examples

Python simulations and plots help connect theory with quantitative behaviour and model limitations.

Purpose

Why this website exists

Private teaching
