Teaching philosophy

Derivations before memorization.

Lessons emphasize definitions, notation, assumptions and the chain of reasoning behind formulas. The goal is to make the student comfortable with the method, not only with the final answer.

  • Start from the student's real course material.
  • Reduce complex topics to prerequisites and representative examples.
  • Build transferable problem-solving routines.
  • Use computation and visualization when it improves understanding.
