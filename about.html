About

Academic tutoring with mathematical clarity and exam focus.

Koyuncu Academy is led by Batuhan Koyuncu, a physics student and tutor with experience teaching school, exam-prep and university-level students in English, German and Turkish. The work combines clear theory, structured problem solving, digital materials and realistic study planning.

Teaching philosophy

Derivations before memorization.

The teaching style emphasizes definitions, notation, assumptions and the chain of reasoning behind each formula. The goal is to make students comfortable with the method, not only with the final answer.

Lessons are built from the student's current material: lecture notes, homework, exam tasks, lab reports, notebooks or past papers. Complex topics are reduced to prerequisites, representative examples and a repeatable method the student can use independently.
