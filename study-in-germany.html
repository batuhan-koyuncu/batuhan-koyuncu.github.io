Study in Germany Consulting

Educational orientation for Turkish students planning Germany.

This service is for Turkish students who want to understand German higher education, prepare academically and realistically plan the transition to student life in Germany.

Scope

What this consulting can cover

Study-path orientation

Understanding possible education paths, study fields, academic expectations and the difference between school and German university learning.

Academic preparation

Preparation for university-level mathematics, physics, engineering thinking, independent study habits and technical terminology.

Student life in Germany

General student-life guidance about adapting to Germany, university culture, language preparation and practical study routines.

Application preparation mindset

General orientation about preparing documents, timelines and research questions without presenting this as official admission or legal advice.

Language and culture

Discussion of German/English study environments, language preparation and academic communication expectations.

Life in Leipzig and Germany

Future articles may discuss everyday student life, bureaucracy basics, cost awareness, routines and academic culture.

Important boundary

Not legal, immigration or admission advice.

This service is general educational orientation, academic preparation and student-life guidance. It does not promise admission, visa success, scholarships, university placement or official immigration/legal outcomes.
