# Image Sources and Visual Strategy

## Visual strategy

Koyuncu Academy uses a small set of realistic academic images rather than decorative stock photography:

- **Academic workspace:** supports the homepage, student access, registration and contact flows by showing focused study, digital tutoring preparation and private materials.
- **Mathematics blackboard:** supports lesson-model, about and public-resource pages by reinforcing derivation-based physics and mathematics tutoring.
- **Quiet classroom:** supports the tutoring page without implying a large school or named institution.

The selected images avoid fake-smiling corporate scenes, identifiable institutional branding and images that suggest previous employers or institutions.
