# Image Sources and Visual Strategy

## Visual strategy

Koyuncu Academy uses a small set of realistic academic images rather than decorative stock photography. The goal is to make the website feel serious, trustworthy, international and academic without implying affiliation with a specific school or university.

### Homepage strategy

The homepage should use one common brand-level hero image across EN / DE / TR. The common image gives the site a stable visual identity and makes the language versions feel like one coherent platform.

- **Common homepage / brand image:** `assets/img/ka-international-library.jpg`
  - Used for the main homepage hero across EN / DE / TR.
  - Represents focused academic work, international study, tutoring preparation and calm university-style learning.

### Language-specific supporting strategy

After the homepage hero, supporting images can differ by language and audience:

- **English pages:** international, university-oriented, multicultural academic imagery.
- **German pages:** structured Nachhilfe / Abitur / university-preparation imagery with a serious German/European academic atmosphere.
- **Turkish pages:** focused study and study-abroad preparation imagery suitable for Turkish-speaking students and families.
- **STEM / lesson-model pages:** blackboard, mathematics and derivation-oriented visuals.

The selected images avoid fake-smiling corporate scenes, identifiable institutional branding, university logos, and visuals that suggest Koyuncu Academy is officially affiliated with a school or university.

## Future image replacement criteria

When replacing or adding images later, use only legally usable public images from reputable providers such as Pexels, Unsplash, Wikimedia Commons or similar. Download images into `assets/img/`; do not hotlink external files. Every new image must be documented here with source URL, author/provider, license and intended usage.
