# Koyuncu Academy Design System

## Visual Identity
Premium boutique academic brand: calm, rigorous, international and parent-friendly.

## Colors
Ink navy, warm ivory, muted academic blue and restrained bronze/gold accents.

## Typography
Serif headings for academic credibility and system sans-serif body text for readability.

## Layout
Editorial hero layouts, restrained cards, full-width bands and stable navigation.

## Buttons
Primary navy, secondary ivory, ghost for quiet supporting routes.

## Image Style
Premium generated academic images with no logos, no readable fake text and no fake exam questions.

## Tone of Voice
Confident, precise, academically serious and commercially clear. Do not invent credentials, testimonials, prices or outcomes.
