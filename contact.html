Checklist

What to include in your message

For tutoring

  • University, school, course or exam type.
  • Topic, exercise, dataset, report or case study.
  • Deadline, exam date or project timeline.
  • Current level and preferred language.
  • Relevant PDFs, screenshots, notebooks, tasks or lecture notes.

For exam preparation

  • Exam type such as Abitur, A Level, SAT, IB or IGCSE.
  • Subject, topic list and target date.
  • Current level and recent difficulties.
  • Preferred lesson language and available materials.

Privacy note

Email communication

Please do not send highly sensitive personal documents in a first message. Briefly describe your request first; if documents are needed, a suitable sharing method can be agreed later.
