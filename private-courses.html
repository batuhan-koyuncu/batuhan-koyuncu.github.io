Tutoring paths

Separate support for university students and exam-prep students.

The tutoring offer is divided into two clear paths: university-level academic support and university-preparation / school exam tutoring.

University TutoringExam & University Prep

Path 1

University Tutoring

For students who are already taking university courses and need rigorous support with lectures, problem sheets, exams, reports or projects.

Physics and mathematics

Mechanics, electrodynamics, quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, mathematical methods, analysis, differential equations and vector calculus.

Engineering and computing

Electrical engineering, circuits, signals, materials science, programming, Python/Jupyter, LaTeX and reproducible academic workflows.

Data, statistics and business

Data analysis, regression, statistics, reports, presentations, business and taxation case-study reasoning.

Path 2

University Preparation & Exam Tutoring

For school students, parents and exam candidates preparing for the transition into university-level study or international exam systems.

German Nachhilfe and Abitur

Mathematics and physics support for school foundations, homework, class tests and Abitur preparation.

A Level, SAT, IB and IGCSE

Structured exam preparation, topic review, problem-solving strategies and practice with typical question formats.

School-to-university transition

Preparation for university-level mathematics, physics, engineering notation, independent study habits and academic expectations.

How lessons work

From material to method.

  1. Send your course, exam type, topic, deadline and material.
  2. We identify the missing definitions, formulas or strategy.
  3. We solve representative examples step by step.
  4. You leave with a clearer method for similar tasks.

Before the lesson

What to prepare

  • Course, school, university or exam type.
  • Topic, exercise, dataset, report or case study.
  • Deadline or exam date.
  • Current level and preferred language.
  • Relevant PDFs, screenshots, notebooks, tasks or lecture notes.
