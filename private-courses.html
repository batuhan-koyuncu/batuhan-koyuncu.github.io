Tutoring paths

Separate support for university students and exam-prep students.

Koyuncu Academy provides rigorous, step-by-step academic support. Lessons focus on definitions, notation, assumptions, worked examples, exam strategy and transferable problem-solving methods.

Path 1

University Tutoring

For students who are already taking university courses and need support with lectures, problem sheets, exams, reports, computational tasks or projects.

Physics and mathematics

Mechanics, electrodynamics, quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, mathematical methods, analysis, differential equations, linear algebra and vector calculus.

Engineering and computing

Electrical engineering, circuits, signals, materials science, programming, Python/Jupyter, LaTeX, Beamer and reproducible academic workflows.

Data, statistics and business

Data analysis, regression, probability, statistics, reports, presentations, business taxation and exam-oriented case-study reasoning.

Path 2

University Preparation & Exam Tutoring

For school students, parents and exam candidates preparing for the transition into university-level study or international exam systems.

German Nachhilfe and Abitur

Mathematics and physics support for school foundations, homework, class tests, Oberstufe topics and Abitur preparation.

A Level, SAT, IB and IGCSE

Structured exam preparation, topic review, problem-solving strategies, past-paper practice and typical question formats.

School-to-university transition

Preparation for university-level mathematics, physics, engineering notation, independent study habits and academic expectations.

How lessons work

From material to method.

  1. Send your course, exam type, topic, deadline and material.
  2. We identify the missing definitions, formulas, prerequisites or strategy.
  3. We solve representative examples step by step.
  4. You leave with a clearer method for similar tasks and a realistic next-study plan.

Before the lesson

What to prepare

  • Course, school, university or exam type.
  • Topic, exercise, dataset, report or case study.
  • Deadline or exam date.
  • Current level and preferred language.
  • Relevant PDFs, screenshots, notebooks, tasks or lecture notes.
