Workflow

How a lesson works

  1. You send the course context, topic, deadline, current level, preferred language, and relevant material before the lesson.
  2. We identify the exact bottleneck: concept, notation, method, problem-solving strategy, data interpretation, or computation.
  3. During the lesson, we work step by step and keep the reasoning explicit.
  4. Afterwards, you should have a clearer method for continuing independently.

Before the lesson

What to send before the lesson

  • Your university, school, or course name.
  • The exact topic, chapter, exercise, dataset, or case study.
  • Your deadline or exam date.
  • Your current level and what you already tried.
  • Lecture notes, problem sheets, PDFs, screenshots, code files, datasets, or notebook files if relevant.
  • Your preferred language and lesson format: single session, weekly support, exam preparation, or project support.
