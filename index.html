Teaching method

Derivations before memorization.

Lessons focus on definitions, assumptions, notation, worked examples and the reasoning chain behind each formula. The goal is to leave with a method, not only a final answer.

  1. Send your course, topic, deadline and material.
  2. We identify the conceptual bottleneck.
  3. We solve examples step by step.
  4. You leave with notes, strategy and next steps.

FAQ

Can parents contact you?

Yes. Parents can send the school level, topic, exam date and the student’s current difficulty.

Do you help with university problem sheets?

Yes, especially when the goal is to understand the method and prepare for similar exam problems.

No. This is a static GitHub Pages website. Contact first; payment can be arranged externally after the lesson format is agreed.

Send your course name, topic, deadline, current level, preferred language and relevant files. I will use that context to suggest a focused tutoring session.
