Teaching style

Rigorous, step-by-step, visual, and computational.

My aim is not only to get the final answer, but to make the method transparent. I emphasize definitions, assumptions, notation, derivations, physical intuition, and concrete examples. When helpful, I use diagrams and Python simulations to make abstract ideas easier to see.
